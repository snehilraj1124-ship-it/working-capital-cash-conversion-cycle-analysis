# Working Capital & Cash Conversion Cycle Analysis

## Meridian Consumer Products Ltd.

A comprehensive working capital management and Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) analysis of a fictional consumer-products company, focused on identifying opportunities to improve liquidity, reduce cash tied up in operations and strengthen working-capital governance.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Working capital management is critical because a growing business can generate accounting profits while still experiencing cash-flow pressure.

This project analyzes the operating cash cycle through:

- Accounts Receivable
- Inventory
- Accounts Payable
- Net Working Capital
- Liquidity Ratios
- Cash Conversion Cycle
- Working Capital Scenarios
- Cash-Release Opportunities
- Risk Management
- KPI Monitoring

The objective is to determine how efficiently the company converts operating activity into cash.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Analyze historical working-capital trends.
2. Calculate Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).
3. Calculate Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO).
4. Calculate Days Payables Outstanding (DPO).
5. Calculate Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC).
6. Analyze Net Working Capital.
7. Evaluate current and quick ratios.
8. Identify receivables-management opportunities.
9. Identify inventory-efficiency opportunities.
10. Evaluate supplier payment-term optimization.
11. Build CCC improvement scenarios.
12. Estimate potential cash-release opportunities.
13. Develop working-capital targets.
14. Identify working-capital risks.
15. Build a management KPI dashboard.
16. Develop an implementation roadmap.

---

# 🏢 Business Context

**Meridian Consumer Products Ltd.** is a fictional Indian consumer-products company used for this portfolio project.

The company has experienced revenue growth over multiple years, but increasing sales can also increase:

- Customer receivables
- Inventory requirements
- Supplier balances
- Working-capital requirements

The project therefore evaluates whether the company is converting its operating activity into cash efficiently.
